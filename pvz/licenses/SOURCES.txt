Proportional Hor+ mobile scene patch: 2026-11-02.
The engine JS and WASM were rebuilt from the pinned source with a native
600-unit-high camera whose width follows the landscape drawing-buffer aspect.
The WebGL projection uses equal X/Y pixel scale; original sprites and text keep
their proportions. Existing panorama art is revealed beyond the original frame.
Native scene changes anchor selector groups and the board menu, extend backdrop
edges, and adapt Tree of Wisdom scenery while retaining original board rules.
The bank and shovel remain above the original nine-column lawn. Decorative
margins do not accept planting input. Portrait uses the original 4:3 view.
Rendering goes directly to WebGL, without a cropped screenshot compositor.
Input is queued through the native event pump. SDL swap-triggered Asyncify sleeps
are disabled and modal-safe resize prevents competing suspended stacks.
The HTML shell, responsive viewport and Telegram integration are modified locally.
